Regions, endpoints, and why Osaka is special

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTML

About five minutes of reading

A Region is a capacity, latency, and residency boundary. It is not a checkbox you can flip on a live number.

The instance, its numbers, and its logs are Regional. Global Resiliency is the only supported way to link two instances, and only in published pairs: Northern Virginia with Oregon, Frankfurt with London, Tokyo with Osaka.

Osaka is a replica Region for Tokyo. Numbers have to be enabled for Global Resiliency, and delivery through Osaka can take up to 20 seconds. That is a design input, not a footnote.

  • New accounts start at two instances per Region.
  • Quota raises are per Region. Raising Oregon does not raise Virginia.
  • The CCP domain is instance.my.connect.aws. The old awsapps.com domain is documented as going away.
  • Softphone TURN endpoints are Regional. A laptop in another country still talks to the instance Region.

Number on this page: Osaka delivery 20s. Upper figure in the replica notes

Confirm before relying. Prices, quotas, and exam names change. Use the source linked on this page.

Glossary

  • Contact One interaction: a call, chat, task, or email. A transfer usually creates another contact linked by PreviousContactId and NextContactId.
  • Connect Customer The name used from 28 Apr 2026 for the contact center product previously called Amazon Connect.
  • CloudWatch Metrics and flow logs. Alarm at 80 percent of a quota, which is the operations guidance in the email quota note.
  • Kinesis Data Streams The durable stream for CTRs and agent events when more than one consumer must read them.
