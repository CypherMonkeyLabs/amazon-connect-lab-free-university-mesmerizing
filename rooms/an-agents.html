Agent events are the live staffing truth

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTML

About five minutes of reading

The real-time board is a view. The event stream is the feed other systems can trust.

Events include login, logout, status change, and connecting to a contact. WFM products such as Aspect and Eleveo consume this stream for adherence. You enable data streaming and point it at a Kinesis stream. Size the shards for the number of consumers.

ACW duration is derivable from the events. If your occupancy formula needs ACW, this is the source, not a guess from the wall board.

  • A missed logout event leaves someone 'available' in the third-party tool.
  • Status names must match the WFM map.
  • Do not let a test tool consume the production stream without its own iterator.
  • Firehose to S3 is the historical copy. The stream is the live copy.

Glossary

  • Conversational analytics The 4 Aug 2026 name for Contact Lens: transcripts, sentiment, redaction, summaries, and categories.
  • Contact Lens The former name of conversational analytics. The product page says the function remains.
  • Real-time analytics Rules on a live contact, announced 1 Dec 2020. Default quota is 300 concurrent real-time analytics calls.

Config and troubleshooting

Exporting a CSV once a night and calling it real-time adherence.

Contact trace records leave the instance through AWS::Connect::InstanceStorageConfig. ResourceType CONTACT_TRACE_RECORDS and StorageType KINESIS_STREAM is the fan-out path. Contact Lens is an instance attribute plus the recording and analytics block in the flow.

InstanceStorageConfig CfnInstance

CloudFormation study snippet
Resources:
  CtrStream:
    Type: AWS::Connect::InstanceStorageConfig
    Properties:
      InstanceArn: !GetAtt CampusInstance.Arn
      ResourceType: CONTACT_TRACE_RECORDS
      StorageType: KINESIS_STREAM
      KinesisStreamConfig:
        StreamArn: !GetAtt ContactStream.Arn
