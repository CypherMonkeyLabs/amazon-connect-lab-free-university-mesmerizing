Evaluation forms, from a blank page to a rule

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTML

About five minutes of reading

A form can be filled by a person, by a category, or by a model. Activation is still your step.

You can create a form from a sample, from a PDF of at most 2 MB, from a blank form, or from JSON exported by another instance. The create permission named on the page is Analytics and Optimization - Evaluation forms - manage form definitions - Create. Sections and questions can carry weights.

Automation can answer questions from contact categories or from a model that reads the question title, the instructions, and the transcript. Fully automated submission has to be turned on before you activate the form. Activation then asks you to create a rule that chooses which contacts get that form.

  • Sample forms cover agent-handled and self-service interactions.
  • The page names five outcome metrics on sample forms: customer satisfaction, churn propensity, successful sale, and full or partial self-service success.
  • A PDF import comes back as a draft, typically within one minute, for you to review.
  • You can save a draft and leave. Activation is a later step, after preview and validation.

Number on this page: PDF 2 MB. Maximum file when importing a form

Worked example

HTML
  1. Draft. Sample, PDF, blank, or JSON.
  2. Score. Weights on sections and questions.
  3. Automate. Category or model, per question.
  4. Activate. Then the rule that submits it.

What usually goes wrong: Activating a form and expecting every answer to fill itself before automation is on for each question.

Glossary

  • Conversational analytics The 4 Aug 2026 name for Contact Lens: transcripts, sentiment, redaction, summaries, and categories.
  • Contact Lens The former name of conversational analytics. The product page says the function remains.
  • Real-time analytics Rules on a live contact, announced 1 Dec 2020. Default quota is 300 concurrent real-time analytics calls.

Contact trace records leave the instance through AWS::Connect::InstanceStorageConfig. ResourceType CONTACT_TRACE_RECORDS and StorageType KINESIS_STREAM is the fan-out path. Contact Lens is an instance attribute plus the recording and analytics block in the flow.
