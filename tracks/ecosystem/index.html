Ecosystem

Learn it in 5 minutes

HTML

Connect answers the contact. Workforce, quality, emergency location, and the system of record sit beside it, and they only stay honest if they share ContactId.

17 rooms. Open one, then use its quiz.

The contact center is not the whole operation

WFM, quality, emergency location, and the CRM fail quietly when they are bolted on after go-live.

Glossary

A migration argument is usually two definitions of the same word.

Mind map

If you cannot point at the box that owns a function, you will build it twice.

Native forecasting and scheduling

Native WFM is in the product. Third-party WFM is justified when you already live in that suite, not because Connect 'cannot schedule'.

Third-party WFM configuration and faults

The integration is the agent event stream plus a historical extract. If either is late, adherence is fiction.

Recording and screen capture

If the object is not in S3, you do not have a recording, no matter what the flow block said.

Third-party recorders and live media

Some QM suites want the audio while the call is up. That is Kinesis Video Streams, not a second SIP trunk.

Quality management on top of analytics

A score without the recording is an opinion. A recording without a form is an archive.

Configure conversational analytics and fix the empty transcript

Analytics that is not enabled on the contact produces an empty search and a false 'the feature is down'.

E911 for agents on the CCP

A contact-center platform is not automatically an emergency-calling design. In the US Regions where this exists, you still have to store a real address.

CRM configuration and the faults that look like routing bugs

A wrong screen pop is often an attribute bug, not a Salesforce bug.

AI agents: configure, cite, and a way out

An agent who cannot reach a person is not assisted. They are stuck.

Integration maps

A map is a list of seams with a key on each line. A poster of logos is not a map.

Future hooks, not future rewrites

Add the next system on a hook that already exists. Do not scrape the CCP and do not fork the flow for every vendor.

The forecast interval is a one-time choice

Scheduling and capacity planning both wait on a forecast, and the interval cannot be edited later.

Hiring plans and scheduling plans

A hiring plan and a scheduling plan do not read the same forecasts.

Publish the schedule before you talk about adherence

Adherence metrics appear after schedules are published.
