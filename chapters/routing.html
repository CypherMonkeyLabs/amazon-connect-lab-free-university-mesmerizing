Chapter

Routing profiles, queues, and skills

Who can take the contact, and in what order

Start with the queue and the one profile on the agent. Then hours and quick connects. Then proficiency steps, which run only after the contact is queued.

Fundamentals

HTML

A queue waits. One profile links it to agents.

🔀

Queues wait. Profiles decide who is eligible.

A queue with nobody whose profile includes it is a silent parking lot.

🔀

Priority, delay, and concurrency

Lower priority number wins. Delay keeps a second team in reserve. Concurrency is how many of each channel an agent holds.

Working

HTML

Hours, overrides, and the transfer flow on a quick connect.

🔀

Hours and overrides

The check uses the queue's hours and the queue's time zone. Your laptop's clock is irrelevant.

🔀

Quick connects and the flows behind them

A quick connect is a target plus a flow. The flow is where you record, whisper, and set attributes.

Advanced

HTML

Predefined attributes, step timers, and a preferred agent.

🔀

Proficiencies filter inside a queue

A proficiency chooses among agents who already have the queue. It does not replace the queue.

🔀

Routing criteria, expiry, and a preferred agent

Set routing criteria does nothing until Transfer to queue, and it does nothing inside an agent queue.
