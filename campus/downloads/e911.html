E911 for CCP agents

United States agents on the CCP. Test with 933. Do not place a live 911 call from this lab.

  1. Validate the address with the Chime ValidateE911Address API.
  2. Store it on that agent's Customer Profile.
  3. An outbound whisper flow copies it to CivicAddress when the dialed string is 911 or 933.
  4. Direct 911 from the CCP is documented for US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), and GovCloud US-West.
  5. Connect does not transfer a live call to 911 and does not let an agent dial 911 while already on a call.

Source: US emergency calling
