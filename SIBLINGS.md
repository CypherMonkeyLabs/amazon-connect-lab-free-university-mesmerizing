# Sister campuses

Amazon Connect Lab follows the same idea as two other offline campuses: one HTML file, no CDN, cited lessons, and labs that do not need an account.

- UC Lab: `https://github.com/cipher0x9/uc-lab-free-university-mesmerizing`
- AI Lab: `https://github.com/cipher0x9/ai-lab-free-university-mesmerizing`

The visual bar those projects were measured against is the Gemini Gems gallery at `https://cipher0x9.github.io/gemini-gems-free-gallery/site/`.

This campus is the contact-center one. It adds the Foundations track, the Bridge track from CUCM and the other platforms, and the Ecosystem track for WFM, recording, E911, CRM, and agent assist.
